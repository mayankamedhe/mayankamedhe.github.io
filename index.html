Robot Learning & Human-Robot Interaction

I build robots that
understand people.

I’m a first-year master’s student in Robotic Systems Development at the CMU Robotics Institute. In the HARP Lab with Henny Admoni I work on reward learning that lets a robot adapt from human feedback when it hits something it wasn’t trained for. My capstone, sponsored by Nissan, is a Unitree G1 humanoid learning factory manipulation from video of people doing the work.

Before CMU I spent four years at Honda R&D Tokyo, working on perception and interaction for micro-mobility robots that operate around people. Thousands of people used them in public trials.

Selected work

Systems I designed, built, and put in front of real users.

Honda R&D Tokyo2022–2026

Smartwatch-robot interaction

A vision-language control interface that turns spoken intent on a smartwatch into robot behavior for autonomous micro-mobility. Deployed in a public proof-of-concept in unstructured outdoor environments and showcased at IFAC 2023.

5,000+users reached

Honda R&D Tokyo2025–2026

Text-guided map retrieval

Spatial retrieval-augmented generation for vague geospatial language such as "the café near the second crossing". GPS-contrastive CLIP embeddings with an OCR-aware re-ranker, so the robot can localize from a description rather than a coordinate.

+18.6 ppR@1 over SOTA

UTokyo · Sato Lab2020–2022

Gaze-guided video captioning

Where a person looks tells you what matters in a scene. I folded eye-gaze priors into I3D features through attention maps for dense video captioning, and released a gaze-aligned description dataset covering 2.5 hours, 33 viewers, 15 languages.

+24.4%action grounding

Honda R&D Tokyo2024

Pointing-gesture stop estimation

Point at the curb and the vehicle stops there. Stereo triangulation combined with 3D human pose recovers the intended stop position from a single gesture, removing the need for a map pin or a spoken address.

Patentapplication filed

CMU × Nissan2026–

Learning manipulation from human video

Teleoperated demonstrations do not scale on a factory floor. My capstone asks whether a Unitree G1 humanoid can learn manipulation tasks mainly from ordinary human video, retargeting it for vision-language-action policy pre-training.

In progressNissan-sponsored

Honda R&D Tokyo2023–2024

Who to follow in a crowd

Fine-grained pedestrian attribute recognition so a robot keeps track of its own user while leading or following through a busy street, optimized with TensorRT to run in real time on Jetson hardware at the edge.

Real-timeon-device inference
